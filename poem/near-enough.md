# Near Enough

Lines marked ♪ are sung. Everything else is spoken. It is one voice throughout: she speaks when she is
cautious and checking, and sings when she is hoping and dreaming.

This is the locked text (from `draft-4.md`). It is the single source for voice prompts, timing and
captions.
